ACE Data Protection Consulting Ltd (“ACE”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is committed to protecting personal data and respecting the privacy rights of individuals who interact with our organisation.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and protect personal data when you visit our website, enquire about our services, participate in our training activities or otherwise engage with us.

Our processing activities are primarily governed by the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 (NDP Act) and applicable regulations, directives and guidance issued by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Where our processing activities fall within the scope of other applicable data protection legislation, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) or EU General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), we will comply with the relevant requirements.

ACE Data Protection Consulting Ltd is a company registered in Nigeria, providing professional services in data protection, privacy governance, regulatory compliance, risk management, consultancy and training.

ACE operates through its Nigerian-registered company. Our UK virtual office address is not an operational business establishment.

ACE is committed to processing personal data lawfully, fairly, transparently and responsibly.

We seek to ensure that personal data is:

08

Use of Cookies

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They may help websites function properly, remember user preferences, maintain security and provide information about website usage.

Types of Cookies

Depending on the technologies enabled on our website, we may use the following categories:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies These support essential website functions, including security, navigation and services requested by users.
  • Functional Cookies These may remember user preferences and improve website functionality.
  • Analytics and Performance Cookies These may help us understand website usage, identify technical issues and improve performance.
  • Marketing Cookies Where used, these may support the delivery or measurement of promotional content.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Where consent is required by applicable law, we will obtain it before placing or accessing non-essential cookies.

You may manage available cookie preferences through our website controls or browser settings.

Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

Some cookies and similar technologies may involve the processing of personal data. Such processing will be subject to applicable data protection requirements.

Information about the specific cookies and technologies used on our website should be made available through our cookie notice or preference settings.

How Long Do We Keep Your Personal Data?

ACE retains personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, regulatory and contractual requirements.

Retention periods depend on the nature of the information and the purpose of processing.

We consider factors such as:

  • The duration of our relationship with the individual or organisation.
  • The purpose for which the data was collected.
  • Applicable statutory or regulatory retention requirements.
  • Contractual obligations.
  • The need to resolve disputes or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
  • Relevant security and operational requirements.

We maintain or apply appropriate retention arrangements for different categories of information.

When personal data is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to securely delete, anonymise or otherwise dispose of it, unless continued retention is required or permitted by law.

You may contact us for further information about the retention arrangements applicable to your personal data.

15

Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023, and other applicable data protection legislation, you may have rights concerning the personal data we process about you.

These rights are subject to the conditions, limitations and exemptions provided by applicable law.

  1. Right to Be Informed You have the right to receive appropriate information about how and why your personal data is processed.
  2. Right of Access You may request confirmation of whether we process your personal data and access relevant information about that processing.
  3. Right to Rectification You may request correction of inaccurate personal data or completion of incomplete information.
  4. Right to Erasure You may request deletion of personal data in circumstances provided by applicable law.
  5. Right to Restrict Processing You may request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.
  6. Right to Data Portability Where applicable, you may request personal data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.
  7. Right to Object You may object to certain processing activities, including processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing, where applicable.
  8. Rights Relating to Automated Decision-Making Where applicable, you may exercise rights relating to decisions based solely on automated processing, including relevant rights to human intervention or review.
  9. Right to Withdraw Consent Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time.
  10. Right to Lodge a Complaint You may raise a complaint with ACE or the relevant data protection supervisory authority.

Exercising Your Rights

To exercise your rights, contact:

Please provide sufficient information to enable us to identify your request and the personal data concerned.

We may request reasonable information to verify your identity before responding.

We will respond without undue delay and within the timeframe required by the applicable data protection legislation.

Where a request is subject to a lawful limitation or exemption, we will explain the position where appropriate.
