Data Privacy Audit and Risk Assessment.

Data controllers are required to conduct detailed annual audits of their privacy and data protection practices. If their processing activities meet the filling threshold, they must submit a summary of the audit report to the commission.

As licensed Data Protection Compliance Organisation (DPCO),we are legally authorised to assist organisations with their data protection audit process, including the filling of audit reports with the commission. Our approach ensures that businesses not only meet regulatory requirements but also strengthen their data protection frameworks, enhancing trust and reducing compliance risks.

Contact Us All services

Our audit and risk assessment services include:

Conducting data privacy audits and risk assessments to evaluate the design and effectiveness of implemented data protection measures.

Analysing existing privacy programmes to identify gaps and vulnerabilities that could expose organisations to unnecessary risks.

Assisting in the reporting of data breaches in compliance with legal provisions, ensuring organisations are well-positioned to mitigate risks and minimise the impact of breaches.
