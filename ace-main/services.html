Data Privacy Audit and Risk Assessment.

Data controllers are required to conduct detailed annual audits of their privacy and data protection practices. If their processing activities meet the filling threshold, they must submit a summary of the audit report to the commission. As licensed Data Protection Compliance Organisation (DPCO),we are legally authorised to assist organisations with their data protection audit process, including the filling of audit reports with the commission.
