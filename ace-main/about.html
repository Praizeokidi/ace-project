Ace Winning Data Protection Solutions

Ace Data Protection Consulting is a licensed Data Protection Compliance Organisation offering data protection and privacy compliance services for data controllers and processors across EMEA region (Europe, Middle East, and Africa).

We have extensive knowledge and practical expertise in cybersecurity and data privacy laws and regulations.

We provide strategic guidance to ensure compliance and effective risk management in an evolving digital landscape.

Who We Are?

We assist organisations in defining the strategies and procedures necessary to properly manage various types of data.

Our priority is always the interests of our clients; we invest time in understanding their unique needs, operating context, and the opportunities and challenges they face.

Our clients are central to everything we do. By understanding what they value and aligning with their needs, we ensure our approach is always tailored and effective.

What We Offer?

Our expertise covers key regulations, including:

  • Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDP Act) 2023
  • EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • Cybercrimes (Prohibition and Prevention) Act
  • Other relevant statutes addressing emerging cybersecurity and data privacy challenges

Our Core Values

Our core values define who we are and how we operate. We are committed to excellence, consistently going beyond industry standards to exceed expectations. With deep expertise in privacy laws and industry best practices, we deliver top-tier solutions tailored to evolving regulatory landscapes.

Understanding that every business is unique, we prioritize customization, designing services that align perfectly with specific needs and goals. Integrity is at the heart of everything we do, ensuring transparency, honesty, and ethical practices in all our engagements. Above all, we emphasize efficiency, guaranteeing seamless execution and timely delivery to help businesses integrate privacy and compliance effortlessly.
