What's included

A practical resource library for every stage of the DPIA Made Easy Professional Edition process.

Use the resources individually or combine them into a consistent workflow for screening, assessment, treatment, approval, and review.

◉

Determine

DPIA Screening

Identify high-risk processing and decide whether a full DPIA is required before the assessment begins.

◉

Assess

Privacy Risk Assessment

Evaluate privacy impacts, likelihood, severity, existing controls, mitigation measures, and residual risk.

◉

Consult

Stakeholder Consultation

Capture stakeholder views, responsibilities, concerns, decisions, and actions throughout the DPIA process.

◉

Map

Data Flow Mapping

Document how personal data moves through systems, teams, recipients, transfers, and the processing lifecycle.

◉

Implement

DPIA Implementation records

Maintain evidence of decisions, approvals, mitigation actions, owners, review points, and implementation progress.

◉

Support

Supporting templates and worksheets

Use reusable formats for exercises, registers, checklists, action tracking, reporting, and ongoing review.
