What's included

A practical resource library for every stage of the DPIA Made Easy Professional Edition process.

Use the resources individually or combine them into a consistent workflow for screening, assessment, treatment, approval, and review.

◉

Determine

DPIA Screening

Identify high-risk processing and decide whether a full DPIA is required before the assessment begins.

◉

Assess

Privacy Risk Assessment

Evaluate privacy impacts, likelihood, severity, existing controls, mitigation measures, and residual risk.

◉

Consult

Stakeholder Consultation

Capture stakeholder views, responsibilities, concerns, decisions, and actions throughout the DPIA process.

◉

Map

Data Flow Mapping

Document how personal data moves through systems, teams, recipients, transfers, and the processing lifecycle.

◉

Implement

DPIA Implementation records

Maintain evidence of decisions, approvals, mitigation actions, owners, review points, and implementation progress.

◉

Support

Supporting templates and worksheets

Use reusable formats for exercises, registers, checklists, action tracking, reporting, and ongoing review.

Do I need the Workbook before purchasing the Toolkit?

No. The Toolkit is designed to be useful on its own. The bundle is recommended when your team wants both the method and the implementation assets.

Can I use the Toolkit in my organisation?

Yes. The resources are intended to support organisational DPIA screening, assessment, risk treatment, governance, approval, and review processes. Confirm internal adaptation and licensing terms before broad redistribution.

Is the bundle recommended for teams?

Yes. The bundle gives teams a shared method and a common set of working resources, making it the strongest option for capability-building and consistent implementation.

Are updates included?

Update availability will depend on the final product and payment arrangement. ACE will publish the confirmed update policy before purchase links go live.

Who is this Toolkit designed for?

It is designed for Data Protection Officers, DPCOs, privacy consultants, compliance and risk teams, legal teams, project teams, and leaders responsible for accountable data processing.
