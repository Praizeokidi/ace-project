Independent professional review

A second look at your DPIA.

Already completed a Data Protection Impact Assessment? ACE can independently review the documentation and highlight gaps, risks and practical next steps.

For organisations seeking an independent review of an existing assessment.

Knowledge that moves from regulation to action.

What the review includes

A focused, independent assessment.

ACE examines your existing DPIA as a documented assessment and decision record, with attention to the areas that support defensible, accountable privacy risk management.

01

Risk assessment

Review how privacy risks have been identified and assessed, including their relevance and treatment.

02

Mitigation measures

Consider the proposed safeguards and whether the documented measures address the risks identified.

03

Necessity & proportionality

Review the rationale recorded for the processing and its relationship to the stated purpose.

04

Accountability & records

Check the clarity and completeness of the decision trail and supporting documentation.

05

Gaps & recommendations

Identify areas requiring further consideration and provide practical recommendations.

06

Review summary

Receive a concise summary of observations to help your organisation plan appropriate next steps.
