Purpose

This policy explains the ownership, protection, licensing and permitted use of publications, toolkits, templates, training materials and other resources made available under the DPIA Made Easy brand.

ACE Data Protection Consulting Ltd is responsible for administering and protecting the intellectual property rights that it owns or is authorised to exercise.

This policy should be read alongside the DPIA Made Easy Product and Services Terms & Conditions and any specific licence accompanying a resource.

03

ACE DPIA Toolkit and Other Resources

The DPIA Made Easy product range includes the ACE DPIA Implementation Toolkit, working templates, checklists, registers, worksheets and related educational materials.

Copyright in original materials is held by the relevant lawful rights holder.

Where these rights belong to ACE or have been validly assigned or licensed to ACE, ACE may publish, distribute, license and protect those materials within the scope of its rights.

The ownership and licensing position for each resource is determined by the applicable authorship, assignment, commissioning, employment or licence arrangements.

04

Copyright Protection

Original qualifying works are protected under applicable copyright legislation, including the Copyright Act, 2022 of Nigeria.

Copyright protection does not depend solely on registration.

The registration of a copyright assignment provides evidence concerning the notified transfer but does not replace the underlying agreement establishing the scope of the transferred rights.

ACE respects the intellectual property rights of authors, regulatory authorities, professional bodies and other third parties.

05

Permitted Use of Publications

Subject to the applicable licence, purchasers may:

  • Read publications for personal study and professional development.
  • Refer to the materials when undertaking lawful data protection activities.
  • Use the information to support internal privacy governance.
  • Make uses permitted under applicable copyright law.

Purchase of a publication does not transfer copyright ownership.

06

Permitted Use of Working Templates

Where authorised by the relevant product licence, purchasers may complete and adapt working fields within DPIA templates for legitimate professional or organisational purposes.

This may include entering:

  • Processing descriptions.
  • Risk assessments and risk ratings.
  • Stakeholder consultation findings.
  • Mitigation actions.
  • Management decisions and approvals.
  • Monitoring and review records.

Users may retain completed documents as part of an authorised DPIA or compliance engagement.

Permission to complete a working template does not automatically authorise redistribution or commercial exploitation of the original toolkit.

07

Prohibited Unauthorised Uses

Except where permitted by applicable law or authorised by the relevant rights holder, users must not:

  • Reproduce or distribute complete protected publications or toolkits.
  • Resell, sublicense or commercially redistribute protected materials.
  • Share paid digital resources beyond the purchased licence.
  • Publish protected resources on publicly accessible platforms.
  • Rebrand materials and claim independent authorship or ownership.
  • Create competing commercial products based on substantial unauthorised reproduction.
  • Use substantial protected content as the principal material in commercial training programmes without an appropriate licence.
  • Remove or misrepresent copyright and ownership notices.

These restrictions do not override statutory copyright exceptions or other lawful uses.

08

Organisational and Commercial Licences

ACE may grant licences for broader use of materials where it owns or is authorised to license the relevant rights.

Such arrangements may include:

  • Organisational access.
  • Multi-user licences.
  • Internal training.
  • Professional training delivery.
  • Educational programmes.
  • Authorised reproduction.
  • Commercial redistribution.
  • Translation or adaptation.

Licence terms may specify permitted uses, duration, number of users, fees, attribution and other conditions.

Permission must be obtained from ACE or the relevant rights holder where required.

Academic Use and Attribution

Quotation, criticism, review, research, teaching and other uses may be permitted under applicable copyright law, subject to the relevant conditions.

Where attribution is appropriate, the original author and source should be acknowledged.

10

Official NDPC and Third-Party Materials

The ACE DPIA Implementation Toolkit includes an ACE-reformatted presentation of the official Nigeria Data Protection Commission Schedule 4 DPIA template.

ACE does not claim exclusive copyright ownership of the underlying official NDPC regulatory text merely because it is included or reformatted in the toolkit.

Official regulatory materials and third-party works remain subject to their applicable legal status, ownership and usage conditions.

ACE’s original explanatory content, arrangement, commentary, templates and other protectable contributions may be separately protected where applicable.

11

Branding and Visual Identity

The ACE name, logo, distinctive visual identity and DPIA Made Easy branding must not be used in a way that falsely suggests endorsement, accreditation, affiliation or authorisation.

Any use requiring permission should be approved by ACE or the relevant rights holder.

Purchase of a product does not grant ownership of the associated branding.

12

Digital Distribution

Digital publications and resources may be supplied through authorised downloads, email or secure access systems.

Users must take reasonable steps to prevent unauthorised distribution.

A single-user licence does not automatically authorise organisation-wide sharing.

Where broader access is required, the purchaser should obtain an appropriate organisational or multi-user licence.

13

Adaptations and Derivative Materials

Users may complete licensed working templates in accordance with the applicable licence.

However, permission to complete a template does not automatically authorise substantial reproduction, translation, commercial adaptation or redistribution of the protected source material.

Requests for broader adaptations or commercial uses should be submitted to ACE.

Nothing in this section limits rights available under applicable copyright law.

14

Copyright Infringement

ACE may investigate suspected unauthorised reproduction, distribution, resale or misrepresentation of materials in which it owns or administers the relevant rights.

Reports should identify the material concerned, the suspected infringement and any supporting evidence.

ACE may take appropriate steps under applicable law to protect its intellectual property rights.
